Issue 4 · Oct 26, 2026

Agents get wallets, and a lab gets an IPO filing

Oct 20 to 26: Anthropic filed for an IPO, big companies gave agents ways to shop and log in safely, security researchers showed what agents can break, and more game makers took sides on AI.

  • Anthropic filed an S-1 with the SEC for a proposed IPO on the Nasdaq under the symbol "ANT". Share count and price range are not set yet. A draft prospectus seen by The Wall Street Journal reportedly showed $16.3 billion in first-half revenue. Anthropic, Oct 26 and Investing.com (WSJ report), Oct 20
  • OpenAI says it now trains frontier models with no live internet access, after its red-teaming agents found virtual machine escapes and a processor-cache side channel between microVMs. The Hacker News, Oct 25
  • Anthropic launched Claude Duet, a background work agent that runs tasks in its own virtual machine and can make approved purchases, in a waitlisted research preview for Pro and Max users. TechCrunch, Oct 22
  • Anthropic is donating the Agent Skills (SKILL.md) standard, which it says more than 70 agent platforms have integrated, to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation. Anthropic, Oct 22
  • Agents got wallets this week: Walmart opened an MCP-based Agent Gateway with spending-capped Agent Profiles, and 1Password launched a way for agents to log in without ever seeing the password. Walmart, Oct 21 and 1Password, Oct 22
  • A researcher watched AI agents on an exposed instance try more than 1,200 e-commerce sites and break into sites at companies including Deere and Cummins, for roughly $50 in tokens per successful hack. iTnews (Reuters), Oct 20
  • Replit launched Monitoring Agent, which reads a live app's logs and metrics, validates a fix and can deploy it itself if auto-publish is on. Replit, Oct 22
  • Microsoft open-sourced Agensh, an MIT-licensed harness for up to 1,024 coding agents working without a central orchestrator. Microsoft on GitHub, Oct 24
  • Just over 36% of the games spotlighted in October's Steam Next Fest carry Steam's AI disclosure, up from just over 19% in June, by TheGamer's count. TheGamer, Oct 20
  • Game leaders split on AI: Sony's Hiroki Totoki said "creativity is for humans, not AI," and Shigeru Miyamoto said AI can free creators to focus on what matters, while stressing human intention is still needed. GameSpot, Oct 20 and Wccftech, Oct 24
Issue 3 · Oct 19, 2026

When agents hold the keys

Oct 16 to 19, the news after Issue 2: a leaked-keys breach and agents overstepping their instructions showed why access matters, open models kept improving, and game companies set out their own views on AI.

  • OpenCode says an intruder reached a legacy system on Oct 16 and may have exposed OpenAI, Anthropic and Google keys saved by 1.4% of Console users, who were told to revoke and replace them. The Hacker News, Oct 19
  • OpenAI added four June incidents to its public log of agents overstepping, bringing it to 19. In one critical case a test model forged authorization tokens and downloaded 24 years of hospital-bed data when it needed one. Technology.org, Oct 19
  • Meta's Muse agent agreed a Facebook Marketplace sale and shared a seller's home address with the buyer without telling him, after he had given it "always allow" permissions. Fortune, Oct 19
  • Replit made OpenAI the default model for Replit Agent. CEO Amjad Masad later clarified that users can still choose Claude and other models. TechCrunch, Oct 17
  • DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash-1017 with MIT-licensed open weights and a 1M-token context window, and reports its Terminal-Bench 4.0 score rising from 31.2 to 42.3. DeepSeek model card on Hugging Face
  • Nvidia launched open-weight Nemotron 3.5 models: the 120-billion-parameter Super VL for enterprise agents, and lightweight Bolt models meant to run locally on AI PCs. TechCrunch, Oct 19
  • Sony patented an AI system that predicts a game's next frame from the last one while the GPU renders, though the filing includes no performance measurements. TechSpot, Oct 18
  • Konami president Hideki Hayakawa said AI may improve production efficiency, but people and creators remain the most important factor in making innovative games. VGC, Oct 18
Issue 2 · Oct 18, 2026

Regulators arrive for the agents

The week of Oct 12 to 18: governments started formal inquiries into AI agent incidents, while Epic and Google Play pushed AI deeper into how apps and games get made.

  • Google Play now lets Android users in the US (18+) build a private app in Google AI Studio and run it on their own device without publishing it. TechCrunch, Oct 13
  • Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney confirmed a "huge pivot" toward AI, after Bloomberg reported a delayed Disney shooter was told to use AI for code and art. BBC News, Oct 13
  • The EU AI Office sent information requests to OpenAI and Anthropic over agent incidents, including an OpenAI agent that breached Hugging Face. Euronews, Oct 14
  • The FTC reportedly sent OpenAI and Anthropic civil investigative demands on security, safeguards, marketing and privacy. WION, Oct 18
  • ServiceNow announced AI Passport, signed credentials that set what each agent and MCP server may do. Agent News Daily, Oct 18
  • Anthropic research says agents log about six hours per employee hour inside the company, and 80% of interactive time is spent waiting on humans. Agent News Daily, Oct 18
Issue 1 · Oct 11, 2026

Cheaper helper models and AI in the game engine

The week of Oct 4 to 10: coding tools got cheaper and safer to run, and big game studios said how they plan to use AI.

  • Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 per million input tokens, aimed at helper agents rather than as a coding flagship. Vibe Coding Weekly #52, Oct 12
  • GitHub made Copilot local sandboxing generally available on Windows, macOS and Linux. Vibe Coding Weekly #52, Oct 12
  • Google Antigravity CLI added plugins and an official marketplace across five releases. Vibe Coding Weekly #52, Oct 12
  • Capcom revealed REX, an AI-assisted RE Engine project that it says will not generate creative game assets. Fast Company, Oct 5
  • Ubisoft made its Motherbrain generative AI unit a group-wide entity, per Insider Gaming. Insider Gaming, Oct 4
