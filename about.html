About

A young project, built in the open

Agentwise is a small, independent site made by a young builder who is learning to make games and apps with AI, with a parent's help. It's new, so there are no big claims here: just guides we've tested, sources you can check, and honest opinions.

Who it's for

Beginners, teens, parents and teachers who want to build something real with AI: a small game, a quiz, a tool for themselves. You don't need to know how to code. You do need to be willing to test things and ask one question at a time.

How we make content

  • We use AI tools to help research, write and code, and we check the output. Guides include a finished code file that's tested automatically in a real browser before every update.
  • News items and prices link to their original sources with dates. Prices are marked verified, partial or unverified.
  • Rankings and "best for" notes are our opinion, labeled as opinion.
  • We don't publish made-up numbers, reviews or testimonials. If we don't know something, we say so.

How Agentwise makes money

Right now it doesn't. There are no ads and no paid placements.

  • Affiliate links: there are none on the site today. If we add any later (links where we earn a small commission if you buy), each one will be clearly labeled next to the link, and they will never change our rankings or opinions.
  • Agentwise Pro: a planned paid pack (the Game Builder Pack and extra project guides). It's not for sale yet; see the Pro page. A parent will own and run any store or payment account.
  • Free stuff: if a company ever gives us free credits or access, we'll say so on the pages that mention that company.

Privacy

  • The only personal information we collect is the email address you type into a signup form, plus which form or link you came from (for example "pro-waitlist"), so we know what's working. Signups are saved to a private Google Sheet through a Google Apps Script form handler.
  • We use it only to send the newsletter or the Pro launch email. We don't sell or share it.
  • No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics scripts on this site.
  • Under 13? Ask a parent before signing up.

Safety

We don't post personal details about the people behind Agentwise. Our guides remind you never to paste passwords, keys or personal info into AI tools, and to check each tool's age rules.

Found a mistake?

Prices and features change fast. If something here is wrong or out of date, open an issue on the site's GitHub repository (GitHub requires users to be 13+) and we'll fix it.
