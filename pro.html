What Pro is planned to include

Main pack

Game Builder Pack: 100 prompts

100 prompts to make browser games with AI, in 10 categories of 10, in the order you need them. Every prompt card has a goal, the copy-paste prompt with [fill-in] blanks, what to check, and an "if it breaks" follow-up.

Guides

More full project guides

Longer builds in the same format as our free guides: step prompts, checkpoints, common bugs, and the complete tested code with a playable demo.

Bonuses

Cheat sheet, worked example, safety page

A printable one-page prompting cheat sheet, one complete game built start to finish with the pack (source included), and a safety page: what not to paste into AI tools, age rules, and asset licenses.

The 10 prompt categories

  1. Game ideas and design
  2. First playable (HTML and JavaScript)
  3. Controls and physics
  4. Scoring, levels and saving
  5. Art with AI (with licensing notes)
  6. Sound and music
  7. Debugging and fixing
  8. Agents and automation, used safely
  9. Polish and publish
  10. Learn while you build

Questions

How much will it cost?
The planned price for the Game Builder Pack is in the $5 to $9 range, as a one-time purchase rather than a subscription. That's a plan, not a promise: the final price will be shown clearly before anyone can buy.
When does it launch?
When the pack is finished and tested. We don't have a date to promise yet. Waitlist members get one email when it's ready.
Who sells it?
Agentwise is run by a young builder. A parent will own and manage any store and payment account, and purchases will happen on an established store platform, not on this site.
Can I try before buying?
Yes. The free Starter Kit prompts are part of the pack, and the free project guides show exactly what the guide format looks like.
Refunds?
The refund policy will be published on the store page before launch.
Does Pro guarantee I'll make a game?
No. AI tools vary and make mistakes. The pack gives you a tested path and fixes for common problems, but you do the building.
